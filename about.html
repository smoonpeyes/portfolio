About me

I design systems that help people make confident decisions in complex, high-stakes environments.

In brief

I'm a Principal Product Designer with 20+ years of experience building enterprise tools and consumer products for high-stakes environments: airline operations, government benefits, healthcare, and grocery at scale. I work at the intersection of complex systems and human needs, translating regulatory constraints, union contracts, and technical complexity into experiences people can actually trust.

I'm known for building design infrastructure that outlasts any single project: accessibility programs, design systems, intake processes, review workflows, and for leading cross-functional teams toward outcomes that matter.

What I do

I specialize in the hard design problems: the ones with legacy systems, expert users, compliance requirements, and organizational complexity baked in. My work spans both sides of the enterprise/consumer divide — I've designed internal tools for Southwest Airlines flight crews and planners, and consumer experiences for millions of H-E-B grocery shoppers.

At the staff level, my work is as much about shaping the conditions for good design as it is about the design itself.

How I work

Front-load understanding

Before I open Figma I want to know the business constraints, the regulatory environment, the user's mental model, and where the current system is failing. That investment pays off — I design fewer things wrong.

Strategic and hands-on

I can facilitate a stakeholder alignment session in the morning and spend the afternoon in Figma working through interaction states. I don't treat strategy and craft as separate disciplines.

Building with AI

I coded this portfolio using Claude Code, and I use Figma Make for component prototyping and dev handoff. I'm not precious about tools. I use whatever helps me do better work faster.
